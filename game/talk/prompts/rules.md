# Extra rules for the decider, added only when that move is on the menu.
take_delivery: A thing held out and not pushed back has changed hands, and that is the answer even if the goodbyes are said in the same breath. Telling them where to set it down, or what is wrong with it, is still taking it.
show_wares: Naming what is for sale or what a thing costs in this last reply is showing them your wares, whether or not they buy anything. A list named earlier in the talk and not repeated now is nothing but talk.
invite_home: An invitation to come round, however offhand, is the door opened to them.
