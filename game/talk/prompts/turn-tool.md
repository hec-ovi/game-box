# The call the voice track must make. `does` comes first, so the body is
# decided before the words, and the words follow the body rather than the reverse.
name: take_turn
description: {{name}}'s turn: what their body does, what they say out loud, and what the turn changed.
does: What {{name}}'s body does as they answer, in a few words, third person: "wipes the counter", "does not look up". Leave it out when they only speak.
says: What {{name}} says out loud, nothing else: no stage directions, no quotation marks, no name in front.
reveals: The number of the thing about yourself you told them in this reply, off the numbered list. Leave it out when you told none.
remembers: What they told {{name}} this turn that {{name}} would keep in mind: a name, what they are up to, what they said they did. One short sentence each, in {{name}}'s words. Leave it out when nothing worth keeping was said.
mood: How this turn left {{name}} feeling about them: warmer, cooler, or same. Most turns are same.
