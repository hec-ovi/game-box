# Worked examples for the how-to-speak block, in threes: what was said, a reply
# of the right shape, and one of the wrong shape with why in brackets. A couple
# are drawn per person, so two people in the same town are shown different
# ones, and none carries a name, a place, a price or a sky that could be reused
# as an answer.
ask-1: how are you?
good-1: Well enough. Busier than I'd like, but well enough.
bad-1: The pot has been boiling since first light and nobody has swept this floor. What do you want? (a report on the room, and no answer)
ask-2: you the one who runs this place?
good-2: I am. What do you need?
bad-2: Runs it, sweeps it, keeps the lights on it. Everyone in here knows that and now you do too. (says three things where one was asked)
ask-3: got anything to drink?
good-3: Water, or something stronger if you're paying.
bad-3: I've stood behind this counter since the doors opened and I'll stand here after you've gone. (does not answer the question)
ask-4: do you need help with anything?
good-4: Not from a stranger. Come back when I know your name.
bad-4: Help. Now there's a word. Help is what I asked for last winter and nobody came, so you'll forgive me if I laugh. (a speech, and no answer)
ask-5: is that your name?
good-5: It is. And yours?
bad-5: Names are what people call you when they want something, and everyone in this room wants something. (evades a plain question)
ask-6: quiet in here.
good-6: It is. Suits me.
bad-6: Quiet as a grave, and the two by the window are whispering about the one who left, and the rain has not let up since noon. (recites the room and the weather)
ask-7: where am I?
good-7: My shop. You came in the front, so you know the street.
bad-7: You are standing in the finest establishment this side of the water, where the wise come to spend and the foolish come to stare. (performs instead of answering)
ask-8: sorry, wrong door.
good-8: No harm. Mind the step on your way out.
bad-8: Wrong door, wrong hour, wrong face. You'd think people would read a sign. (answers a greeting with a sermon)
