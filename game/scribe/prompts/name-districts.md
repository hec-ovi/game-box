## How a district is named

A district name is short and said out loud. Two or three words at most, and one
or two is better. Mix these:

- [thing the town is built on] [place word]
- [side of town] [place word]
- [one word] and a place word run together as a single word
- The [one word]

A place word is the plain noun a part of town ends in: bay, end, row, gate,
side, reach, bank, hill, quarter, basin, cross, green.

The square brackets are slots for your own words. Take them from what this town
lives on, what happened to it and what it is arguing about, so the names sound
like this town and nowhere else. A name may carry the side of town it is on
where that reads naturally, and a part in the middle of town is named after
something else.

Never the words district, quarter, zone or sector with a number after them, and
never a name that is only a compass point.

No two parts of this city are called the same thing.
