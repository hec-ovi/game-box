## The doors

Each of these is a real building already standing, with its height, its
footprint and the street its door is on. Use them: a twelve storey building on
an avenue is not a corner shop, and a two storey one down a lane is not the
city's main station.

A door marked settled is one the town needs: what it is was decided before this
call, and it is written back as it stands.

{{buildings}}

Say what every one of them is, under the label it was given. Decide it out of
what this town lives on, what happened to it and who is arguing over it, so
these doors are the town's own and not a row that would fit any city.
