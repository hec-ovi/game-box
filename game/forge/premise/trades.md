# What a town lives on

One of these is picked for every town, from the kind of town its theme reads
as. It is the first half of the premise: why the place is here at all, the noun
the town is often named after, and the buildings that follow from the trade.

Format: `## handle`, then `- key: value` lines. `fits` is one or more flavours
or `any`. `more`, `fewer` and `must` are `@gb/world` building kinds: more of,
fewer of, and one the town has to contain whatever the mix rolls.

## the seam
- fits: frontier
- lives: the ore still coming out of the hill
- word: Seam
- more: workshop, warehouse, bar
- fewer: office, cafe
- must: workshop
