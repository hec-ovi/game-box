    Your settings

    Left blank, every television plays the schedule its town writes for itself. A source that will not play leaves that schedule running. It is kept in this browser and never written into a city, so a file you send somebody carries nothing of it.

    Add to a city

    A pack is what somebody built onto this city later. It applies to this one city and no other, and it comes back with what it added. Grow writes the next block of buildings and hands you the pack for it.
