# What happened to it

The second half of the premise: the thing that happened recently enough that
people still talk about it, what it left at stake, the two sides that came out
of it, and what everybody in town knows about it.

The stake is what the main line is about, and the two sides are bound to the
two counters the main line forks between, so a choice in a quest is a choice
about this.

Format: `## handle`, then `- key: value` lines. `fits` is one or more flavours
or `any`. `more`, `fewer` and `must` are words of charters, and `declares`
names a kind of place from `src/premise/places.ts` the town is founded with. `side` is
`who they are / what they want`, and repeats. `known` repeats.

## the trade went
- fits: coastal, industrial, frontier
- happened: the trade that built this place stopped coming, and nobody has said out loud that it is not coming back
- stake: who ends up holding the empty sheds
- more: warehouse, bar
- fewer: hotel, office
- must: warehouse
- side: the families who worked it / the sheds kept shut until the work comes back
- side: the receivers holding the debt / the whole lot sold on before it rots
- known: half the buildings on the water belong to somebody who has never been here
- known: the last load out went eleven months ago and people still date things from it

## the fire
- fits: any
- happened: a fire took four streets in one night, and the rebuilding has been going on ever since
- stake: who gets to say what goes up on the burnt ground
- more: workshop, clinic, chapel
- fewer: hotel
- must: clinic
- side: the people who lost the streets / their own houses back on their own ground
- side: the developers rebuilding it / something on that ground worth more than what burned
- known: the burnt ground is still fenced, and the fence is a shortcut everybody uses
- known: nobody has been told how the fire started

## the sickness
- fits: any
- happened: a sickness went through the winter before last, and the town is still short of the people it lost
- stake: whether the town admits it is smaller than it was
- more: clinic, chapel, apartment
- fewer: restaurant, hotel
- must: clinic
- side: the people who kept the surgery open / the town counted honestly and helped
- side: the council / the whole thing behind them and the visitors back
- known: the ward at the clinic has been full since before the winter
- known: a third of the doors on some streets have nobody behind them

## the new money
- fits: any
- happened: money arrived from outside two years ago and has been buying the place one street at a time
- stake: who the town is going to belong to
- more: office, hotel, cafe
- fewer: workshop, house
- must: office
- side: the people who were already here / rents that somebody who works here can pay
- side: the buyers / every corner of it worth what they paid for it
- known: three of the best corners changed hands in one week and nobody local was told
- known: the rent on a room has doubled since the spring

## the strike
- fits: industrial, coastal, frontier, neon
- happened: the people who do the work stopped doing it eight weeks ago, and neither side has moved since
- stake: whether the work starts again on the old terms
- more: bar, workshop, chapel
- fewer: office, hotel
- must: bar
- side: the ones out on strike / the terms they had back, in writing
- side: the owners / the gates open on Monday, whoever walks through them
- known: the gates have been chained since the second week
- known: somebody is paying for the food outside the gates and nobody will say who

## the flood
- fits: coastal, agrarian, alpine, plain
- happened: the water came up further than anyone alive had seen it, and the low half of town is still drying out
- stake: whether the low streets are worth saving
- more: clinic, warehouse, workshop
- fewer: apartment, office
- must: clinic
- side: the low streets / the wall built and their houses kept
- side: the high streets / everybody moved up and the low ground given back
- known: the tide mark is painted on the chapel wall, above the door
- known: the pumps have run every night since March

## the find
- fits: frontier, alpine, agrarian, industrial
- happened: somebody found something worth money under the town, and the whole place has been arguing about it since
- stake: who has the right to what is under the ground
- more: workshop, bar, hotel
- fewer: chapel, cafe
- must: hotel
- side: the people who live over it / a share for the street it runs under
- side: the company with the paperwork / the claim worked before the season turns
- known: there are more strangers in town than there are beds
- known: two families have stopped speaking over one boundary line

## the shutdown
- fits: neon, industrial, plain
- happened: the network the town ran on went dark for nine days, and a good deal of what it held never came back
- stake: who gets trusted to hold the town's records now
- more: office, shop, clinic
- fewer: station, warehouse
- must: office
- side: the people who kept paper / everything written down where a person can read it
- side: the operators / the system back up and everybody on it again
- known: nine days of records are simply gone, and nobody agrees what was in them
- known: half the town is being asked to prove things it proved years ago

## the road out
- fits: any
- happened: the road out was cut through eighteen months ago, and the town has been on the way to somewhere ever since
- stake: whether being on the way is worth what it costs
- more: hotel, cafe, station, shop
- fewer: house, chapel
- must: station
- side: the people who trade with the traffic / more of it, and faster
- side: the old streets / the traffic put round the town rather than through it
- known: the freight comes through at night and nobody on that street sleeps well
- known: two shops that had stood thirty years shut in the same month

## the arrival
- fits: any
- happened: a few hundred people arrived at once, with nowhere arranged for them to live
- stake: where the people who came are supposed to go
- more: apartment, clinic, market
- fewer: office, restaurant
- must: apartment
- side: the ones who arrived / somewhere to live that is not temporary
- side: the streets that were here first / the arrangement kept to what was agreed
- known: the old station hall has people sleeping in it
- known: the market has doubled in size and half of it is new
