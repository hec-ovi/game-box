A flat photographic material scan of a dark grey precast concrete wall panel, worn and grimy, photographed straight on with the camera perpendicular to the surface so the concrete fills the whole frame edge to edge and nothing else is visible. The frame covers two metres by two metres of real wall: fine aggregate pocking, shallow chips, hairline cracks, chalky efflorescence, thin rust weeping and soot staining, all spread at the same density across every part of the frame, with no single crack, stain or patch distinctive enough to be recognised twice. Lit by completely flat even diffuse light from straight ahead, with no cast shadows, no specular highlights, no vignette, no brighter side, and sharp focus from corner to corner. Colour is neutral desaturated charcoal grey with faint warm rust and cool grey variation only. A seamless tiling texture whose pattern continues off all four edges.
